## 🌟 Overview

**Nebula Client** is the most advanced, all-in-one automation client engineered specifically for **Hypixel SkyBlock**. Built with human-like behavioral mechanics, Zero-Ping Etherwarp capabilities, and over **23+ advanced staff check failsafes**, Nebula lets you automate everything—from end-game slayer bosses to complex rift parkours—completely risk-free.

## 🔥 Key Features

### ⚔️ Combat & Slayers
* **Slayer Automation**: Full support for Revenant (TNT dodging), Sven (wall clipping fix & puppy handling), Tarantula (flay skipping, bat handling), and Voidgloom (hit-phase weapons, summons, beacons, laser dodging, Nukekubi targeting).
* **Smart AOTV / AOTE**: Instant target teleportation to dramatically boost kill rates per hour.
* **Combat Presets**: Pre-configured routes, areas, and mob filters for hassle-free grinding.
* **Event Macros**: Diana Mythological Event farming and Pelt Hunter (Galatea Island with ZPEW support).

### ⛏️ Mining & Commissions
* **Packet Mining & Tick Gliding**: Blazing-fast block breaking across all mining zones.
* **Commissions Auto-Solver**: Fully automated Dwarven & Glacite Tunnels commissions.
* **Gemstone & Mithril**: Advanced route management, heat control, Titanium detection, Precision Miner support, and auto-refueling.
* **Fossil Excavator**: Automated pattern solver and reward collection in the Research Center.

### 🛡️ Next-Gen Failsafes
* **23+ Anti-Check Systems**: Real-time staff check detection to protect your account.
* **Humanized Reactions**: Mimics real player actions, mouse delays, and natural movement patterns.
* **Instant Cloud Updates**: Instant protection patches pushed as soon as new staff checks are identified.

### 🎣 Advanced Fishing

## ⚡ Getting Started

1. **Download & Install**: Place the client binary into your mods folder.
2. **Inject / Load**: Launch your supported Minecraft client and load Nebula.
3. **Configure**: Open the in-game GUI (Default key: `RIGHT SHIFT`) to customize your routes, failsafes, and macro presets.
4. **Enjoy**: Sit back and let Nebula handle the grind!
